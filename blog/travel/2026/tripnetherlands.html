Travel 2 - Road to the Netherlands

1 day trip my ASS

Yup, even trying to take a train was incredibly chaotic for me.

For more context about how the trip was possible, read the context page

Back to Friday 17th

Picture this, It's the Friday you have to go home, and get dropped of at the station before ~7 AM more or less. Shortly after arriving, the bus arrives.
You get on the bus, because trains are non functional where you were, and just chug along and alone, your bags in the cargo, listening to music
You arrive to the train station a few hours later (~8 AM more of less-ish), and oops! Your train is cancelled!

That's exactly what happened to me. My train got cancelled and I only learned about that AFTER arriving to the station.

Oh no.

So my train is cancelled, and I'm in a train station, with my bags, just figuring out what to do.
You could think "Okay, just take the next one???" but thing is, It's an hour later, and the schedule does NOT allow for THAT much of a difference. So I just have to replan everything in the trip, and in the end, somehow manage to make an itinerary that's a bit tight, but should work, right????

So I just get my bags, get on the next train, and chug along to Milano Centrale, and before long, well, I get there! And wait for the next train in the itinerary
The train gets there in time, leaves in time, and I'm on track to probably barely make it to the station to change trains
Then the train stops. Just a dead stop, right on the track. For a few minutes, running behind. You could think "Oh it's probably not gonna be that bad"

Then the train I had to take left the station. Right as mine arrived to it. AND IT WAS ON THE NEXT TRACK

By now it's also afternoon, and I was quite hungry. Of course, I kind of did NOT plan about lunch, but I would've been in a train technically!

Except now I wasn't. I am hungry, stuck in a VERY EXPENSIVE COUNTRY, and still too far from my destination

After some help from my mother, I managed to board a train at around 4 PM, which took me from Switzerland, to Germany, where, after a LOT of time, I arrived at 2:49 AM to an station in Germany (Duisburg).

My train to the Netherlands didn't leave until 5 AM, so I just decided that I'd sleep in a hotel, and after a lot of talking with the hotel staff via Email, I managed to get a room! (God bless the recepcionist that answered my pleas for help, they were the best)

Oh god, FINALLY

Finally, after a good rest, even longer thanks to the fact I could take trains later on the morning (Like 11 AM), I was able to just get up, and keep going until arriving to the Netherlands, at around 3-4 PM, and to my friend's place soon after.

It was mostly a chill journey from there, with just a tiny bit of stress over changing platforms in time, and also not understanding what the intercom was saying, but that quickly became normal for me after everything I went through.

Once I got to my friend's place, we hung out for a bit, talked, and after that, I got to shower, and sleep on their couch for that night

Of course, I didn't really know what was coming, and you won't see it either, until next time!

So yes, that indeed was quite the trip. Just know that I was moving aaaaalll my luggage around with me as well, so it was even worse

Regardless, next up is just the Netherlands, and my week there!

Join me for my next adventure soon!
