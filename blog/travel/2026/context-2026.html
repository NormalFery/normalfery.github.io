Hello there! If you are here, you are probably new to the 2026 travel blog, so i'll try to sum up the most common questions regarding how I'm even doing my travel!

How?

DiscoverEU, and help from my parents.

To be more precise, DiscoverEU is a European Union program that allows people that are/will be 18 to get a free train pass for a month, along with, in case you live VERY far from your starting point (Eg, the canary islands), you get a flight to, and from Europe!

Now, while this does sound nice, it is a contest-type of thing, where only selected people may get it (~40kish in the whole union), and each country has a quota!

I actually tried to get it before, but I wasn't selected on that round. Luckily enough, I was able to try again on the spring rounds, and actually got it!

Of course, DiscoverEU ONLY covers trains, and planes. Everything else, like food, places to stay, or other things, must come out of pocket.

That's where my parents come in! Thanks to their help, I was able to afford to get places to stay, food, and such! Of course, this came at a cost, but thanks to both of them splitting, they were able to afford it.

Why?

Well, originally it was just to see my boyfriend. I've always wanted to go to Italy to meet him! However, upon knowing I could stay up to a month in Europe, I decided to stretch stuff instead! Where I'll be meeting many friends, and will be going to more than one country!

Along with that, I also wanted to know how trains feel like, how it is working on them, and, in fact, I'm writing this right now on the train to Olten!

It feels rather weird, but also nice. Though I do have 5 minutes to get from Platform 7 to 10, and on my train
