Travel Entry 1 - Italy

Chaotic at the start, and the end.

Hi everyone! Fery here with the first of, hopefully only, 3 travel blogs! One per country I'm vissiting!

Just for a bit of context, make sure to read the context page, as that'll contain most, if not all the info regarding "How did you even get to go to Europe?" or "Aren't you too broke to go on vacation?"

Now, onto the blog!

A messy arrival

Now, with chaotic, you could've been thinking "Oh, maybe he means his plane had some turbulence or something", but actually, it was worse.

For context, I had to take two planes in order to even get to Italy. TFN to BCN. And BCN to MXP. That sounds good, and it was! I didn't mind the layover, so all was good!

However, while on the MXP flight, the captain made an announcement, that where, due to a storm, we'd have to take a longer route, staying up to 40 minutes later than usual on the air, due to the storm. Now, of course, that wasn't good at all for me. Why?

My train would arrive like under 30 minutes after the original landing time.

So of course, on the plane, I started rerouting everything! Moving the train, changing stuff, until finally, after landing, I took the first train from the airport station, to Milano Centrale.

Once there, I had to wait around an hour, to take the next few trains. Take into account, I was supposed to be where my bf would pick me up at 10 PM.

I got there at 12 AM.

Of course, that felt a bit like shit, but also, it helped me test my skills for a future issue that'd pop up soon.

While on the trains and bus, I was also getting calls from the place I was staying at, because, of course, I was taking longer to arrive.

Luckily enough, the host was very nice, and understood my issues after I machine translated fro English to Italian what's been going on.

Once I got there, I got a tour of the "apartment" I was going to call home for a week, and then proceeded forward to eat the pizza Clancy brought me, and fall asleep on the bed.

A fun Saturday

After that mess of an arrival, I woke up at around ~8:30 AM, and then, after realizing that I was tired as fuck, and had nothing to get for breakfast, aside from some things Clancy had got me, aside from the Pizza from last night.

So I went back to sleep, until after 11 AM, where I did a round of calls, making sure my parents knew I was alright!

After that, I started unpacking my bags, getting out everything for the week, just to have everything sorted out and ready for the week.

Afterwards, I had to go out and get some groceries, mostly so I'd have something to eat in the future, even if I didn't have a pan to use yet, or such.

Once I went to get said groceries, and went uphill, dying to the heat of the afternoon, I just kinda...

ate some leftover pizza from last night.

Then, after figuring out how the shower worked, specially given how weird it was, I managed to take a nice, warm shower!

After that, I got ready, and at ~3 PM, went out to do some sightseeing with Clancy, and his mom!
We just went around the town, and I got to see a lot of things that day!

Sunday, and the rest of the week

On sunday, I had more of a "Chill day", where I just got my bearings more completely, and was pretty much along that entire day... It was a bit sad, but oh well

Then, on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday, I went to his house for most of the day! We hung out, he made lunch for me, and it was very nice! I also got to keep hugging him, and just be with him :3c

Sadly, we did have to say bye, which wasn't really that good, but oh well :<

Thursday, the last actual day

This was my last day in Italy, and I had to do a lot of things, such as pack my suitcases, prepare everything, and worry about my dirty clothes. So at around 8 AM I woke up, and simply walked for ~1km to the laundry place

Once in the Laundromat, while sweating because it was already hot, and it was uphill, I started doing the laundry while asking my mom what I should do. Funnily enough, though, I didn't have enough cash, so I had to walk to the nearest ATM and back to get some cash, to continue doing everything!
Finally, once I finished washing all the clothes, at 10 AM, I headed back home, sweating, and finished preparing

Now, picture this. I'm just packing my stuff, and decide "Hey, I may as well pack my spices and stuff I bought!", and I pack it up on my bag. Then, after moving the bag a bit, you hear the sound of something spilling in the bag...

My garlic spilled all over my bag.

Yup, and it got all over my switch's dock, my clothes, and such! I couldn't really even wash it that much, so I just gave up on that, and finished packing, leaving the spices and such at my "apartment" and just choosing to rest for most of the day

~8:45 PM

I was at my place all day, before Clancy told me to go out for ice cream Gelato, and while scared a bit (yk, gluten), I decided to go with him! He and his mom parked near where I was staying, and we just walked around in the quiet of the night, it was... quite nice.

Each of us got their own Gelato, and I had a lot of extra money on the laundry card I got, so I just... gave it to him!
Now, you may be thinking "man, this would be a perfect moment for them to make out" and I wholeheartedly AGREE, but past ME didn't think of that, and I'm so upset about that now, but I can't do anything about it

In the end, we said goodbye, and they took me to my place, then they left, and I went to bed

Friday, time to go.

I woke up at around 5 AM that morning, Friday, July 17th. I had pretty much everything packed up, so I just took my last Italian shower, wore some comfortable clothes for the day, and at around 7 AM, Clancy was waiting with his car to take me to the station.

I got my 2 bags, and backpack in his trunk, and he just drove me! After that, we got to the station, and while that was ALSO a good "make out" moment, I DIDN'T THINK OF THAT, and just got a kiss from him, then got my bags, before he left!

And that's the last I saw of him physically...
